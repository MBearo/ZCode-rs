[Cross-session delivery notice] {{subject}} {{was}} held by that session{{details}}. NOT delivered: its ZCode has not seen {{it}}. Do not report {{it}} as delivered, do not wait for a reply, and do not resend while held. The usual cause is that the two sessions run in different permission modes: a terminal session then asks its user to approve, but a non-interactive session cannot ask, so there the hold expires undelivered unless the modes come to match. A session can also be set to hold every message. Another notice follows on release, denial or expiry. Tell your user what is held and why, or choose another approach.
